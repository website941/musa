# 01. Organisation Profile & Statutory Constitution
## Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (NHS Trust)

- **Sector:** UK Public Healthcare & National Health Service
- **Founded:** 5 July 1948 (Merged Foundation Trust established October 2017)
- **Headquarters:** Cobbett House, Oxford Road, Manchester M13 9WL
- **Regulator:** Care Quality Commission (CQC) & Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)
- **Statutory Basis & Governance:** NHS Trust Board of Directors & Council of Governors, accountable to NHS England & CQC
- **Tagline:** "Delivering Outstanding Patient Care, Clinical Research & Digital Health Services Across Greater Manchester"

### Executive Mission Overview
Manchester University NHS Foundation Trust (MFT) is one of the largest acute NHS trusts in the UK, operating 10 hospital sites including Manchester Royal Infirmary, Royal Manchester Children’s Hospital, and Saint Mary’s Hospital. Its digital patient portal and web services coordinate outpatient bookings, electronic prescriptions, clinical trial information, and emergency department wait-time telemetry for over 750,000 residents.
